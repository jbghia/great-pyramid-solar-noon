# Paper and software mapping

- Solar roots, daily transits and nighttime bounds: `pyramid_solar_alignment.py`.
- Catalogue rankings, historical targets and sampled/refined residuals: `stellar_alignment_search.py`.
- Daily-transit inclination sensitivity: `queens_chamber_solar_robustness.py`.
- Internal regression checks: `tests/test_pipeline.py`.

Manuscript tables and figures must be generated and checked from the stated executed model. Transcribed values do not establish this. A compact Meeus calculation is not the same model as the Skyfield pipeline. No independent historical numerical reproduction is claimed by this mapping.
