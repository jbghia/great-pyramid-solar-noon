# Publication checklist

- Verify survey provenance and adopted inputs.
- Archive catalogue, kernel, versions, settings and hashes.
- Execute all stages; independently verify historical positions and transits.
- Test convergence, endpoints and coupled sensitivity.
- Generate and review every paper table and figure from the stated model.
- Review author metadata, disclosures, licensing and third-party rights.
- Cite the exact reviewed snapshot; archive it before assigning a DOI.

Internal tests do not establish architectural intention or replace astronomical validation. Maintaining this checklist creates no release.
